---
title: "Concentric Radius"
description: "Nested rounded corners that share a center point: inner radius equals outer radius minus the gap between them."
category: "craft"
---

Two nested rounded rectangles only look right when their corner curves share a center. Because the inner element sits closer to that center by exactly the padding between them, its radius must be smaller by the same amount:

```css
.outer {
  --padding: 8px;
  --inner-radius: 8px;

  border-radius: calc(var(--inner-radius) + var(--padding));
  padding: var(--padding);
}

.inner {
  border-radius: var(--inner-radius);
}
```

Give both elements the same radius and the inner corners look pinched, a subtle wrongness most people feel but can't name. iOS applies this everywhere (icons in folders, cards in sheets), which is part of why its nesting feels effortless.
