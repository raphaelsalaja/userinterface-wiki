---
title: "Overshoot"
description: "When an animated value passes its target before settling, giving motion a sense of momentum and life."
category: "motion"
---

Overshoot is what happens when a moving object carries too much momentum to stop exactly at its destination, it sails slightly past, then corrects. In the physical world almost everything overshoots a little; in interfaces, a touch of it makes motion feel energetic instead of mechanical.

The honest way to get overshoot is a [spring](/glossary/spring) with moderate damping, the amount of overshoot falls naturally out of the physics:

```tsx
<motion.div
  animate={{ scale: 1 }}
  transition={{ type: "spring", stiffness: 400, damping: 15 }}
/>
```

You can fake it with an easing curve whose control points exceed 1 (a "back" ease), but the fake version always overshoots by the same scripted amount regardless of context, which is exactly the tell that separates it from real spring motion.

Use it sparingly. Overshoot draws attention, so it belongs on the focal element of an interaction, not on every element at once.
